Premade

Privacy Policy

Updated October 20, 2026

Premade has no accounts and no ads, and sells nothing. Here's what it handles and where it goes.

  • On your PC. Your settings, LP history, game history, notes, avoid list, clips, game videos, custom games and test recordings stay on your PC, in the app's storage, Videos\Premade and Documents\Premade. Clips by voice and enemy timers listen with a speech model that runs on your PC; what you say isn't sent anywhere. The combo trainer reads only League's ability, spell and item keys and right clicks, only in Practice Tool games while it's ticked, and keeps only your combos and their times.
  • Your screen. Premade reads your shared screen on your own PC to find your champion on the minimap, and the other team's champions there too if you turn on a callout, the jungle clock or "List who's off my minimap" on the Overlay page; for those it reads your screen by itself during Summoner's Rift games. It also looks for League's surrender vote box there, to show your chance to win beside it. With "How I placed with each augment" on the Overlay page, it reads your screen by itself during TFT games too, for the names on the augment cards you're offered. Only your spot on the map goes to the other players in your room. Your screen itself isn't sent anywhere, unless you turn on "Let teammates in my room watch my screen while they're dead" in Voice settings: then, while a friend in your room on your team in the same game is dead and watching, your screen goes to them the way voice does (encrypted, never through our server), and stops when they're back.
  • Voice and room chat go directly between the players in a room, encrypted. When a direct link isn't possible, they can pass through a relay run by PeerJS, which can't listen to them. Our server only introduces players to each other; it never hears voice or sees chat. The phone remote pairs the same way. Enemy timers go to the others in your room the same way, and so do the custom games you play to the end and their 1v1 results (each team's Riot IDs and champions, and who won), which everyone in the room keeps on their PC for the inhouse ladder.
  • Our server (on Cloudflare) counts rooms and players each day, for Settings > Our server. A player is counted as a code made from their internet address and a random value that's thrown away each day, so no address is kept. The counts are kept for 60 days. For a locked room it checks a code made from the password (never the password itself) when someone joins, and forgets it once everyone has left.
  • Who has Premade. When Premade opens, and again when any of this changes or it's used on a new day, it tells our server a random code it made for your device, its app version, the name you use in voice rooms and the Riot IDs of the League accounts Premade has seen there. Our server keeps them with when Premade was first and last opened on that device, so whoever runs Premade can see who has it. Nobody else can see the list. Turning off "Let whoever runs Premade see I have it" in Settings > App stops it. A device that doesn't open Premade for a year is dropped from it.
  • Phone notifications. If you turn on "Tell me when a match is found or my squad calls" on the phone remote, our server keeps your phone's push address (from Google's, Apple's, Mozilla's or Microsoft's push service) and the keys your phone makes for messages to it, with your remote's pairing code, for up to 4 phones a code, until you turn it off or the push service says the address has ended. A match found carries no data: your phone writes "Match found!" itself. A squad's call or ping carries its words (who, and the room or what happened), encrypted so only your phone can read them.
  • Squads and pick'em. If you make or join a squad (Your group, on the Voice page), our server keeps, under the squad's code: each member's name in Premade, a random id for each member, the pings they want, the phone remote's pairing code of members who have the phone remote on (so calls reach their phones; it never goes to the other members), the last call with its answers (shown for 6 hours) and the pick'em guesses (the newest 200 matches). Leaving the squad takes you out of it, and it's deleted when the last member leaves. The pick'em's matches come to our server from LoL Esports' schedule, so your PC doesn't contact LoL Esports.
  • League chat and your status. Premade reads and sends your League messages and party invites through League's own client on your PC; they don't go through our server. With "Show my Premade room" on, Premade puts your room's code in your League status, which League shows your friends.
  • Discord. If you paste a Discord webhook link in Settings, your game results and clips go from your PC straight to that Discord channel, under Discord's privacy policy. The link stays on your PC.
  • Riot lookups. When you open your profile or look up a Riot ID, our server asks Riot's API for that player's public account, rank, top champions, current game and match history, and passes it back. It keeps the rank it saw (Solo/Duo and Flex, the highest and latest each season) with the player's Riot account id, so profiles can show past seasons. Your internet address is used for a moment to limit lookups and isn't kept.
  • High elo statistics come from Riot's match data for the top ranked games in Korea, Europe West and North America, and for Emerald and Diamond games in Europe West and North America. They keep no names or account ids: each player in a game gets a code made from their account id and a secret value of our server's, so a champion's one-tricks can be found, but the code can't be turned back into an account. To find those games, our server keeps Riot's current list of each region's Challenger and Grandmaster players' account ids, and of the first players in each Emerald and Diamond division, replaced every 6 hours. For Champions to try, it keeps the 8 champions each Challenger and Grandmaster player has the most mastery points on, under the same code, for up to 30 days.
  • Ladder places. Once a day, our server reads Riot's Solo/Duo ladder for Korea, Europe West and North America to count how many players are in each rank, and from Master up how many at each LP. It keeps only those counts, with no names or account ids.
  • Pro players. Once a week our server reads the public player list on Leaguepedia (lol.fandom.com, shared under CC BY-SA) for pro players' Riot IDs, and keeps each one's Riot account id, name, team and League server, so champion pages can show pros' solo queue games under their name and team. A player Leaguepedia stops listing is removed two weeks later.
  • Other services. The site and app updates are hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages), fonts come from Google Fonts, the voice library from jsDelivr, champion pictures from Riot's Data Dragon, and the TFT page's Team Planner champions and item recipes from CommunityDragon. Each sees your internet address when the app loads from it, under its own privacy policy.
  • Removing your data. Everything on your PC goes when you uninstall Premade or clear its data. To have your saved ranks or your device's line in who has Premade removed from our server, open an issue at github.com/Rayn16/Premade.
