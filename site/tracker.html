Minimap Camera Tracker

Checks that the app can see your minimap: the white camera box and the champion icons with your team's blue ring. Live, it also shows which icon it follows as you; until it knows, it uses the camera box.

You can also paste a screenshot with Ctrl+V or drop images anywhere on the page.

Screen

The page finds your minimap on its own, at any Minimap Scale. If the dashed square is off, drag a new one around the minimap.

Minimap

Gold box: the camera box the app found. Blue circles: champion icons with your team's ring. Gold dot and dashed circle: the spot used as you and its hearing range, about 1,350 game units. Dots: where you were over the last minute (live only).
